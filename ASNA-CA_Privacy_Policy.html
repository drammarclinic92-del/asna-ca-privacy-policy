Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

ASNA-CA – Your Important Reminder respects your privacy. This application helps users organize daily reminders, religious reminders, personal tasks, and important appointments. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

The application does not require account registration or sign-in. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or phone number to use its reminder features.

The application does not collect or transmit your contacts, photos, audio recordings, or precise location.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Information you choose to enter may include:

  • Daily and religious reminders and their schedules.
  • Personal tasks and recurring appointments.
  • Vehicle details and maintenance records.
  • Doctor appointment details and optional notes.
  • Brush replacement reminders and their dates.
  • Language and application preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device using the application's private storage. The current version does not provide cloud synchronization or upload your reminder records to our servers.

3. Notifications and Permissions

The application requests notification permission to deliver reminders you choose to enable. It may also use exact-alarm scheduling permission to schedule reminders at selected times and a device-restart permission to restore scheduled reminders after a reboot.

These permissions are used for reminder functionality and are not intended to collect personal information or track users. You can manage permissions through your device settings.

4. Internet and External Content

The application may retrieve promotional content, such as an advertisement image or its configuration, from a developer-managed GitHub repository over HTTPS.

When this content is requested, the hosting provider may process technical connection information, such as your IP address and request metadata, according to its own privacy policy.

The current version does not integrate the Google AdMob SDK or behavioral advertising and analytics SDKs. If the application introduces additional advertising or analytics services in a future version, this policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated.

Promotional content may contain links to external websites. When you open those links, the privacy policy of the external website applies.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Reminder information stored locally is not uploaded to or shared with our servers through the application's reminder features. The external content hosting provider may process technical request information when promotional content is retrieved.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Reminder information and preferences remain on your device while the application's data is present. You can delete this information through your device's application settings or by uninstalling the application. This may permanently remove locally stored reminders and records.

7. Children's Privacy

ASNA-CA is intended for general use and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to reduce unauthorized access to information. Since reminder data is stored locally, users are also responsible for protecting their devices and device access credentials. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when application features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised update date.

لا يتطلب التطبيق إنشاء حساب أو تسجيل الدخول، ولا تحتاج إلى تقديم اسمك أو بريدك الإلكتروني أو رقم هاتفك لاستخدام وظائف التذكير.

لا يقوم التطبيق بجمع أو إرسال جهات الاتصال أو الصور أو التسجيلات الصوتية أو الموقع الجغرافي الدقيق.

2. المعلومات المحفوظة على جهازك

قد تشمل المعلومات التي تدخلها بنفسك:

  • التذكيرات اليومية والدينية ومواعيدها.
  • المهام الشخصية والمواعيد المتكررة.
  • بيانات السيارة وسجلات الصيانة.
  • تفاصيل مواعيد الطبيب والملاحظات الاختيارية.
  • تذكيرات تبديل الفرشاة وتواريخها.
  • إعدادات اللغة والتفضيلات الخاصة بالتطبيق.

تُحفظ هذه المعلومات محلياً على جهازك باستخدام مساحة التخزين الخاصة بالتطبيق. ولا يوفر الإصدار الحالي مزامنة سحابية أو رفع سجلات التذكيرات إلى خوادمنا.

تُستخدم هذه الأذونات لتشغيل وظائف التذكير، ولا تهدف إلى جمع المعلومات الشخصية أو تتبع المستخدمين. ويمكنك التحكم في الأذونات من إعدادات جهازك.

قد يجلب التطبيق محتوى تعريفياً أو ترويجياً، مثل صورة إعلان أو إعداداته، من مستودع GitHub يديره المطور عبر اتصال HTTPS.
