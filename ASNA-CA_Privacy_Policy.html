ASNA-CA Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

ASNA-CA is an alarm and reminder application. This Privacy Policy explains how ASNA-CA handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

ASNA-CA does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or other personal information in order to use the core alarm and reminder features.

2. Alarm and Reminder Data

Information you enter into the application for alarms and reminders is stored locally on your device to provide the application's functionality. We do not intentionally transmit your alarm or reminder content to our servers.

3. Internet and Third-Party Content

The application may use an internet connection to retrieve application content or configuration when needed. Third-party services may have their own privacy practices and policies. ASNA-CA does not intentionally send your personal information to third parties for advertising or sale.

4. Advertising

If advertising is displayed in the application, advertising services may process information according to their own privacy policies and the settings available on your device. Any advertising-related data practices will be disclosed in the Google Play Data safety information as applicable.

5. Data Security

Because alarm and reminder information is primarily stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device and operating system. We do not claim that any electronic storage method is completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a server-side account containing your alarm or reminder data. You can remove locally stored application data by deleting the alarms/reminders within the app where supported, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the application from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

ASNA-CA is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised update date.
